Combat AI Bot Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Combat AI Bot ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy is designed to be simple and clear: Combat AI Bot does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.

Combat AI Bot is a virtual AI friend designed for short, private conversations to help you share feelings and receive emotional counsel. Your privacy is fundamental to our design.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

We believe that your conversations are your own. Therefore, we have built the App to operate without collecting any personal information. We do not collect:

  • No Personal Information: You do not need to create an account to use Combat AI Bot. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifiers.
  • Conversation Data: To provide AI responses, your chat messages are sent to our servers for real-time processing by our pre-trained language model. This data is processed ephemerally (in-memory) and is immediately discarded after the AI response is generated. We do not store, log, or use your conversation data for any other purpose, including model training.
  • No Location Data: The App does not collect or track your geographical location.
  • No Usage Data: We do not collect analytics data, such as how you use the app, features used, or time spent.
  • No Device Information: We do not collect information about your device, such as device type, operating system, or unique identifiers.
  • No Identity Verification: We do not require any form of identity verification.

3. How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect any information, we do not use it for any purpose.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share your data with any third parties. Your conversation data is sent only to our secure servers for processing and is immediately discarded. It is not shared with or sold to any external entities.

5. Data Security

We are committed to protecting your data. Communication between your device and our servers is encrypted. While your conversation data is processed on our servers, it is never stored, which minimizes security risks. The data is processed in-memory and discarded immediately after use.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Your privacy is an inherent right. With Combat AI Bot, you don't need to manage complex privacy settings because your data is not collected in the first place. You have the right to use the app anonymously and with complete privacy.

7. Data Retention

We do not retain any data, as no data is ever collected.

8. Children's Privacy

Combat AI Bot is designed to be safe for users of all ages, including children. Our AI is configured to provide only appropriate and safe content, and it will refuse to answer or generate content that is unsuitable for children. While the app is child-friendly, we still do not collect any personal data from any of our users, including children. Parents and guardians can be assured that their children's privacy is protected as no information is ever stored.

9. International Data Transfers

This is not applicable, as we do not collect, store, or transfer any data across borders.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. Since we do not collect your contact information, we cannot notify you directly. Please check this page periodically for updates.
